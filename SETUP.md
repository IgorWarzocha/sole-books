# Set up your UK books

Work through these phases with the user. Resume from the unticked phases, using existing records rather than asking again. Tick a phase only after its actions are verified. Record any inapplicable step and the reason in `state.md`. Missing evidence or a blocked connection is not completion. Keep this file until every phase is complete, then delete it from the user's working branch.

## Phases

- [ ] **1. Establish a safe home.** Confirm this is a private repository and a personal working branch created from `uk`. Never add personal details to `uk` or `demo`, and never start real books from the fictional demo. Read `AGENTS.md`, `state.md`, `todo.md` and `.agent/skills/uk-bookkeeping/SKILL.md`, including its tax setup reference. Confirm that the user's business and intended use fit this edition before promising coverage.

- [ ] **2. Gather the basics.** Ask in a short conversational batch for legal and trading names, business address and contact details, work sold, trading start, UK tax circumstances and business structure, and the first tax year or date to cover. Ask whether Universal Credit is relevant. Find out what already holds their books, invoices, payments and evidence, what they want imported, and which tools they want to keep using. Ask only for information not already established. Agree how invoice payment instructions will be provided. Do not request banking passwords, authentication codes, National Insurance numbers or tax-account credentials in chat or this repo.

- [ ] **3. Resolve the recording requirements.** Follow the tax setup reference in the bookkeeping skill. Research missing public requirements through current HMRC and GOV.UK sources, using the user's actual activity and circumstances. Establish accounting basis, tax-year coverage, registration, filing and payment dates, VAT, Making Tax Digital and evidence retention. Record dated sources and decisions in `state.md`. Ask for private facts or professional review where public research cannot decide. If VAT, MTD or other requirements exceed this repo's scope, agree the required compatible software and what this repo will do alongside it. Do not claim that CSVs or an MCP connection alone meet submission or digital-link requirements. If UC is relevant, read the skill's UC reference and obtain actual assessment and reporting dates, payment dates, calculation scope, MIF, deductions and opening losses from the user's evidence. Do not infer an award or enable an unsupported calculation. If UC is not relevant, record that and leave its ledgers empty.

- [ ] **4. Agree imports and tool boundaries.** Offer files, exports, existing MCP connections or manual records according to what the user actually has. Inspect available capabilities before promising an integration. Agree each source, accounts, coverage dates, cutover date and which system owns each record, including whether this repo replaces or complements another tool. Use read-only access where possible. Ask before connecting services, granting access, installing integration tooling or writing to another system. Keep credentials in the tool's secure mechanism. Review field mappings and a small sample before bulk import. Record currency conversion evidence, gross receipts, fees, refunds, invoice identifiers, tax-year boundaries and duplicate detection. Reconcile overlapping sources rather than adding bank deposits and processor receipts twice. Record the supported repeat-import or manual refresh process. Do not invent a connector or leave a selected import silently unfinished.

- [ ] **5. Populate and connect the records.** Fill `trader.csv` from confirmed details. Create the agreed UK tax-year folders from this edition's headers and align the default year with the agreed starting view. Import only evidenced records, retaining source references and reconciling totals and gaps. Record business shares and claimable amounts, keeping unresolved treatment visible. Align invoices, document status, remaining projections, recurring costs and rate changes. Establish opening cash balances only from evidence. If UC applies, reconcile its periods separately from tax totals and align its actual and projected inputs. Replace template text in `state.md` with the user's confirmed scope, system ownership, import coverage, obligations and evidence workflow. Replace the starter `todo.md` with their actual next actions. If starting fresh, explicitly record that no history was imported and what opening balances remain unverified.

- [ ] **6. Verify and hand over.** Check imported records against source counts, totals, balances and coverage. Run `make check` for every populated year using `TAX_YEAR=YYYY-YY`. Install dashboard dependencies if needed, start Glance with the documented localhost command and verify the agreed tax year, GBP, identity and totals. Ensure the dashboard's date-based year selection has the required ledgers. Verify UC configuration against its evidence when applicable, without presenting an estimate as an award. Confirm that the user understands any incomplete reconciliation or external compliance workflow. Record how to add receipts, reconcile, refresh imports and update linked tools in `state.md`, with ongoing actions in `todo.md`. Nothing is sent, filed, reported or paid during setup without direct approval. Once every phase is ticked and setup blockers are resolved, preserve the durable decisions in those files and delete `SETUP.md`. Keep the conditional `AGENTS.md` trigger for future template updates.
